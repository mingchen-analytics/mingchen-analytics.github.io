BUSINESS ANALYTICS · STATISTICAL MODELING

Exploring What Drives Spotify Track Popularity

Wake Forest MSBA · Team Analytics Project

Excel Descriptive Statistics Hypothesis Testing Regression
QUESTION

Using a large Spotify track dataset, our team examined how audio characteristics such as danceability, energy, loudness, and related features were associated with track popularity.

APPROACH

We cleaned and summarized the data, examined distributions and visual patterns, built probability and inference analyses, and then used multiple linear regression to test how well the available audio features explained popularity.

MY CONTRIBUTION

My contribution included Excel-based data cleaning and descriptive analysis, hypothesis testing, regression modeling, and interpretation of the results, with a focus on communicating what the model could—and could not—support.

KEY INSIGHT

After removing tracks with a popularity score of zero, the regression still explained only about 7% of the variation in popularity. Rather than treating the low R² as a failed model, we interpreted it as evidence that audio features alone provide limited predictive power.

89,741Tracks analyzed
~7%Popularity variation explained by the regression model

Business implication: The project reinforced the importance of model limitations and variable selection. A stronger popularity model would likely require information beyond the audio characteristics available in this dataset.

COMMERCIAL AWARENESS

Where analytics is moving — and what still matters

Across both sports and business, analytics is becoming faster, more accessible, and increasingly AI-assisted. The opportunity is not simply to produce more metrics. It is to connect richer data and better tools to decisions that people can understand, validate, and execute.

SPORTS ANALYTICS · 2025–26

From box scores to movement intelligence

The NBA’s new Inside the Game platform with AWS shows how quickly sports analytics is moving beyond traditional event data. Its optical tracking system captures 29 points on each player’s body 60 times per second, supporting AI-powered measures such as Gravity and tools such as Play Finder that can search similar movement patterns across games.

My takeaway: The next advantage is not just access to more data, but the ability to translate spatial and tracking data into coaching, front-office, and fan-facing decisions.

Source: NBA × AWS partnership →

BUSINESS ANALYTICS · 2026

AI is becoming part of the analytics workflow

In Power BI, Copilot can now support natural-language analysis, report creation, and DAX generation. That lowers the barrier to exploring data, but Microsoft also emphasizes semantic-model preparation, access controls, and reviewing AI-generated outputs before using them.

My takeaway: As tools make analysis easier to generate, analyst value shifts toward asking the right question, preparing trustworthy data, validating results, and explaining the decision behind the output.
