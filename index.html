ABOUT

From evidence to action

Across my career, one question has remained consistent: how can analysis lead to better decisions? Studying Chemistry at National Taiwan University trained me to approach problems systematically and evaluate evidence carefully. In carbon-fiber bicycle product development, I learned that technical information creates value when it can be translated across teams and moved toward execution.

Basketball made that lesson more immediate. As a collegiate coach and video coordinator, I built Excel-based scouting and possession-tagging workflows, led a six-member student analysis team, and used data and film to support game strategy. Building a Mandarin-language basketball analysis platform that grew to 200K+ subscribers and 32.7M+ lifetime views also taught me how to make complex ideas clear and useful to nontechnical audiences.

Now, as a Wake Forest MSBA candidate in the Sports Analytics Concentration, I am strengthening my skills in statistics, programming, modeling, and business problem-solving. I am pursuing business analytics and sports analytics roles where I can combine structured analysis, communication, and decision support to improve performance and execution.
